AsliRate

AI · Product · SMB procurement

A live web app that ranks supplier quotes by what each order really costs in rupees, not by the price printed on the quote.

  • Reads PDFs, WhatsApp screenshots, Excel rate lists and pasted text
  • Ranks 2 to 6 suppliers by real landed cost
  • Counts GST credit, freight and minimum order quantities
  • Flags expired quotes and missed delivery dates

06 / About me

The story so far

My journey has moved across finance, operations, strategy, technology and entrepreneurship.

01

Numbers

I started with commerce: a B.Com (Hons) from the University of Delhi, with internships at three startups along the way (marketing analytics at ConvertCart, student outreach at MentorBoxx, the founder's office at Cric-Crak).

Then I spent two years at KPMG Global Services on statutory audits and financial reporting for multinational clients. That is where I learned to work with complex information, solve problems systematically and get things done across teams.

02

Operations

After KPMG I wanted to see a business from the floor. At Simanjo Foods I worked across production, procurement, inventory and process development.

03

Strategy, technology and AI

Today, at Masters' Union, I am exploring how businesses are built, scaled and transformed, and building things myself. AsliRate, a supplier-quote comparison tool for small Indian businesses, is now live on its own domain.

I am most interested in strategy and consulting, especially in consumer and D2C businesses, where decisions about product, pricing and positioning show up quickly in the real world.

I want to become someone who can look at a business, understand the problem, and help figure out what to do next.
