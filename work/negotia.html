Negotia

An AI-powered negotiation copilot designed to help users prepare for and navigate negotiations more effectively.

StatusLive on Google AI Studio
My roleBuilt by me
CoversVendor, salary, investment and freelance negotiations
Open Negotia ↗

The problem

Most people walk into a negotiation knowing what they want and not much else. They have not written down their alternative, they do not know where the other side's limit probably sits, and they draft the reply in a hurry.

What it does

Negotia keeps each negotiation as a case: who the counterpart is, what the target is, and what stage the talks have reached. For the active case it works out the zone of possible agreement, keeps the BATNA and walkaway threshold on screen, and drafts the next counteroffer.

Inside the app

  • ZOPA detection: your limit, their limit, and the range where a deal exists
  • Strategic anchors: the BATNA option and the walkaway threshold
  • Counteroffer simulator with a drafted counter and an estimated chance of acceptance
  • Leverage score for you against the counterpart
  • Coach tips drawn from what the counterpart has said
  • A final deal report once the case closes

The sample case

The app opens on a Series B negotiation. The founder will give up at most 30% for $25M; the investor wants at least 28%. That leaves a two-point zone to work in. The BATNA is a second term sheet of $22M at an $88M valuation, and the app drafts a counter and suggests when to send it.

The other sample cases are a cloud vendor asking for a 15% price increase, a salary review from ₹12 LPA to ₹15 LPA, and a six-month freelance retainer.
