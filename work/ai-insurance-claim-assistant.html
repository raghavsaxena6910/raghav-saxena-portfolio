AI Insurance Claim Assistant

An AI-enabled insurance claims assistant designed to simplify the claims journey by interpreting policy information, analysing incident details and generating a personalised response for the customer.

TypeConcept and written submission
InputThe policy and the incident details
OutputWhat is covered, what is not, what happens next
Read the submission (PDF) ↗

The problem

Claims are one of the few moments where an insurance customer actually experiences the product, and it is usually the moment with the most friction: unclear policy language, repeated information, and long waits for a decision.

What it does

The assistant reads the policy, takes in the details of the incident, and returns a response the customer can understand: what is covered, what is not, and what happens next.

The approach

The harder question was where automation should stop. A confident but wrong answer costs more trust than a slower human one, so the design keeps the judgement calls visible and does not hide them behind the model.

What I learned

The project helped me think about AI beyond the technology itself, particularly where automation can reduce friction while still keeping the customer experience clear and trustworthy.
