The idea

The project took an economic concept from the classroom and applied it to a business situation involving competitive pricing, strategic reactions and market behaviour.

Quick commerce in India is a useful case for game theory: a small number of players, near-identical offerings, and pricing decisions that are visible to competitors almost immediately.

The argument

The article works through why discounting persists even when it damages every player. Each platform's best response to the others keeps them locked in place.

Rival holds priceRival discounts
You hold priceBoth keep their marginsYou lose orders
You discountYou win ordersBoth lose margin, and nobody can afford to stop

The shape of the argument, simplified

The writing

Writing it was as much an exercise in explanation as in analysis. The concept only earns its place if a reader without an economics background can follow the argument.

What I learned

It showed me how concepts that look theoretical in a classroom can become useful lenses for understanding real business decisions.
