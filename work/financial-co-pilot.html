Financial Co-Pilot

A FinTech concept designed around the problem of making financial information and decision-making easier for users.

TypeProduct concept and deck
ScopeProblem, value proposition, revenue model, unit economics, B2B, regulation
View the deck (PPTX) ↗

The problem

The starting point was a familiar problem: people have more financial data available than ever and still find it hard to decide what to do with it.

The approach

Building the concept meant moving between two views at once: what the user needs in the moment, and whether the economics of serving that need hold up at scale.

What it covered

  • The customer problem and the value proposition
  • The revenue model and unit economics
  • A possible B2B route
  • The regulatory constraints that shape what such a product is allowed to say to a user

What I learned

The most interesting part was understanding how a good product idea has to connect the customer problem with a viable business model.
